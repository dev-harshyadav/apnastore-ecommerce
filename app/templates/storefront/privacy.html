Data & Security

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

APNASTORE values your privacy. This policy outlines how demonstration data is treated in this application.

1. Information Collected

When you place a demonstration order, we collect the customer name, email address, phone number, and physical postal address provided in the checkout form. This data is stored strictly in your local project SQLite database to demonstrate the complete order lifecycle.

2. Security & Session Storage

Shopping carts are managed via secure, HTTP-only server-side sessions. Administrator authentication uses salted and hashed passwords via Werkzeug security primitives. Order confirmation views are session-protected to prevent unauthorized ID enumeration.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Your data is never transmitted, sold, or shared with third-party marketing brokers or analytics trackers.
