Welcome to APNASTORE. This web application is developed as an educational demonstration project for the Prodigy Infotech Internship (Task 03).

1. Demonstration Nature of Orders

Orders placed through APNASTORE are demonstration orders recorded within a local relational database for evaluation purposes. No real financial payment gateways (e.g. credit card processors) are integrated, and no monetary charges will be billed to your account.

2. Product Availability & Pricing

Product details, prices (in INR), and inventory counts are maintained directly within our SQLite database schema to accurately demonstrate full-stack transaction lifecycles, stock reduction, and order record integrity.
